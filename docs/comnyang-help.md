## Getting started

### 1. Buy Comnyang

Comnyang is a one-time purchase of **$3.90** (optionally +$1.90 to buy the developer a coffee). Checkout is handled securely by **Lemon Squeezy**. After purchase, your **license key arrives in the receipt email** from Lemon Squeezy — keep that email, it's your key to everything.

Grab the right build from the [Download page](https://comnyang.com/en/download):

| Your computer | Build | Install |
|---|---|---|
| Mac (M1/M2/M3/M4) | Apple Silicon | Open the DMG, drag Comnyang into **Applications** |
| Mac (Intel) | Intel Mac | Open the DMG, drag Comnyang into **Applications** |
| Windows | Windows | Run the EXE installer |

`[CONFIRM: minimum OS versions — e.g. macOS 12+, Windows 10/11]`

### 3. Wake Comnyang

1. Open Comnyang and **sign in with your Google account**. Your license links to this account so it can follow you across devices.
2. **Register the license key** from your receipt email.
3. **Grant Accessibility permission** when asked — this is how Comnyang sees your keyboard and mouse so it can react to them.
   - **macOS:** System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility → enable Comnyang.

That's it. A cat now lives in your computer.

## What Comnyang does

### Reactions to you

- **Eye Follow** — move your mouse and Comnyang follows it with its eyes.
- **Mouse Hunt** — move the cursor fast and Comnyang tries to hunt it.
- **Purring Pets** — pet Comnyang's head with your mouse and it purrs happily.
- **Keyboard Kneading** — when you type, Comnyang kneads the keyboard with its tiny paws.
- **Overheat Mode** — type too fast and Comnyang turns red with steam puffing over its head.
- **Mochi Drag** — drag to lift it and Comnyang stretches like mochi; shake it and it wobbles.
- **Paper Unroll** — scroll a page and Comnyang unspools a roll of paper with its paws.
- **Peek Mode** — while you watch videos, Comnyang peeks from the screen edge and stays out of the way except for reminders.

### Reminders & focus

- **Stretch Reminder** — set a timer and Comnyang grows big now and then to stretch with you.
- **Drink Water Reminder** — set a timer and Comnyang pops up to remind you to hydrate.
- **Pomodoro Timer** — set focus and break loops; a pixel timer floats next to Comnyang.
- **Message Reminder** — choose a time and message, and Comnyang meows to remind you.
- **Fixed Message** — pin an important note above Comnyang's head so it stays in sight.

- **Your Cat's Pattern** — give Comnyang the same markings as the cat you live with. Custom color and pattern mapping is included with every license.
- **Tell Your Name** — tell Comnyang your name and it will call you by name during reminders and breaks.

## Troubleshooting

**Comnyang doesn't react to my typing or mouse.**
It's almost always the Accessibility permission. On macOS: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility → make sure Comnyang is enabled, then restart the app. If it's already enabled, toggle it off and on again — macOS sometimes needs the nudge after an app update.
